Deliberately empty, which allauth reads as "say nothing": it only adds a message when the
template renders something.

Its default is "Successfully signed in as <name>.", and the page you land on already says
"Hello, <name>" with the account named in the sidebar. A message that repeats what the page
shows is noise, and it costs more than nothing: it trains the reader to skim past the bar where
the real warnings appear (NAF, 2026-09-19).

Signing *out* keeps its message, because the sign-in page you land on is the same page you
would see if the session had simply expired, and the two are worth telling apart.
