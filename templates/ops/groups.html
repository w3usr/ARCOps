Permission levels

A permission level is a set of things its holders may do, and it is what the club grants an account: station and computer access are a separate thing entirely, signed for and approved (Access rosters). An account at no level can do nothing. A sysadmin holds everything whatever level they are at. Changes here take effect at once and are written to the audit log.
