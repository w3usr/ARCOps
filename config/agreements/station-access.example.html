Station Access Agreement

Thank you for joining the club. Members who receive access to the club station agree to the following conditions.

  1. I will treat all people and equipment in the station with care and respect.
  2. I will operate the station according to the regulations that govern the amateur radio service in this jurisdiction, and only within the privileges of the control operator present.
  3. I will not move, remove, or modify station equipment without permission from the advisor or a designated officer.
  4. I will keep the station clean and yield it to members who want to operate.
  5. I will not use or disturb any equipment or space that is not the club's without appropriate permission.
  6. I will report any problem with the station or its equipment to the advisor without delay.

Term

This agreement is in force from its approval until the expiry date the approver sets, and is renewed by signing the then-current version.
